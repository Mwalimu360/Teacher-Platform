INTRODUCTION TO PHYSICS

Form 1 Physics

📖 Notes

1. Meaning of Physics

Physics is a branch of science that deals with the study of matter, energy, motion, forces and their interactions.

2. Importance of Physics

Physics helps us understand natural phenomena and develop technologies used in everyday life. It is applied in transport, communication, medicine, construction, electricity and many other areas.

3. Branches of Physics

Some major branches of Physics include mechanics, heat, optics, sound, electricity and magnetism, and nuclear physics.

4. Physics in Everyday Life

Physics is involved in activities such as walking, cooking, using electrical appliances, travelling, communication and the operation of machines.

5. Scientific Procedures

Physics uses scientific procedures to investigate problems. These include observation, identifying a problem, forming a hypothesis, planning an investigation, collecting data, analysing results and drawing conclusions.

6. Laboratory Safety

A Physics laboratory should be used carefully. Students should follow instructions, handle apparatus correctly, keep the working area clean and report accidents or damaged equipment to the teacher immediately.

NUMBERS

Form 1 Mathematics

📖 Notes

1. Meaning of Numbers

A number is a mathematical object used to count, measure, label or describe quantities.

2. Types of Numbers

Numbers can be classified into different groups. Examples include natural numbers, whole numbers, integers, rational numbers, irrational numbers and real numbers.

3. Rational Numbers

A rational number is a number that can be expressed in the form p/q, where p and q are integers and q is not equal to zero.

4. Irrational Numbers

An irrational number cannot be expressed as a fraction p/q where p and q are integers and q is not equal to zero.

5. Real Numbers

Real numbers consist of both rational and irrational numbers.

6. Number Line

A number line is a straight line used to represent numbers in their order and position.

💡 Worked Examples

Example 1: Classify the number 5 as a natural number, whole number and integer.

5 is a natural number.
5 is a whole number.
5 is an integer.

Example 2: Determine whether 3/4 is a rational number.

3/4 is in the form p/q, where p = 3 and q = 4.
Therefore, 3/4 is a rational number.

Example 3: Determine whether √2 is rational or irrational.

√2 cannot be expressed exactly as p/q where p and q are integers.
Therefore, √2 is irrational.

COORDINATE GEOMETRY

Form 1 Mathematics

📖 Notes

1. Coordinate Geometry

Coordinate geometry is the study of points, lines and shapes using numbers and coordinates on a coordinate plane.

2. Cartesian Plane

The Cartesian plane consists of two perpendicular number lines: the horizontal x-axis and the vertical y-axis. They meet at the origin.

Origin = (0, 0)

3. Coordinates of a Point

A point on the Cartesian plane is represented by an ordered pair (x, y). The first number is the x-coordinate and the second number is the y-coordinate.

4. Quadrants

The two axes divide the Cartesian plane into four quadrants.

Quadrant I: (+, +)
Quadrant II: (-, +)
Quadrant III: (-, -)
Quadrant IV: (+, -)

5. Plotting Points

To plot a point, first move along the x-axis according to the x-coordinate, then move vertically according to the y-coordinate.

6. Gradient

The gradient measures the steepness of a straight line.

Gradient = Change in y ÷ Change in x

7. Straight Line

A straight line can be represented using an equation. One common form is:

y = mx + c

where m represents the gradient and c represents the y-intercept.

💡 Worked Examples

Example 1: State the quadrant in which the point (3, 4) lies.

Both x = 3 and y = 4 are positive.
Therefore the point lies in
Quadrant I.

Example 2: State the quadrant in which (-2, 5) lies.

x is negative and y is positive.
Therefore the point lies in
Quadrant II.

Example 3: Find the gradient of a line passing through (2, 3) and (4, 7).

Gradient = (7 - 3) ÷ (4 - 2)
= 4 ÷ 2
= 2

Example 4: For the equation y = 3x + 2, identify the gradient and y-intercept.

Comparing with y = mx + c:
m = 3
c = 2
Gradient = 3
y-intercept = 2

✏️ Exercises

  1. What is coordinate geometry?
  2. What are the coordinates of the origin?
  3. State the quadrant containing the point (5, -3).
  4. State the quadrant containing the point (-4, -2).
  5. Find the gradient of the line joining (1, 2) and (3, 6).
  6. In y = 4x + 5, identify the gradient and y-intercept.

✅ Answers

1. Coordinate geometry is the study of points, lines and shapes using coordinates.

2. The origin is (0, 0).

3. (5, -3) lies in Quadrant IV.

4. (-4, -2) lies in Quadrant III.

5. Gradient = (6 - 2) ÷ (3 - 1)
= 4 ÷ 2
= 2

6. Gradient = 4
y-intercept = 5

CONCEPT OF MATHEMATICS

Form 1 Mathematics

📖 Notes

What is Mathematics?

Mathematics is a field of study concerned with numbers, quantities, shapes, patterns, relationships and logical reasoning.

Importance of Mathematics

Mathematics is useful in everyday life and in many areas such as science, technology, business, engineering, construction and economics.

Mathematics in Everyday Life

We use Mathematics when counting money, measuring distance and time, calculating prices, planning activities and solving problems.

💡 Worked Example

A student buys a book for TSh 8,000 and pays TSh 10,000. How much change should the student receive?

Solution:

Change = Amount paid − Cost
Change = 10,000 − 8,000
Change = TSh 2,000

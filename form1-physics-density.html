DENSITY AND RELATIVE DENSITY

Form 1 Physics

📖 Notes

1. Meaning of Density

Density is the mass contained in a unit volume of a substance. It describes how closely packed the particles of a substance are.

Density = Mass ÷ Volume

ρ = m / V

2. SI Unit of Density

The SI unit of density is kilogram per cubic metre (kg/m³). Another commonly used unit is gram per cubic centimetre (g/cm³).

Useful conversion:

1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³

3. Determining Density of a Regular Solid

For a regular solid, measure its mass using a balance and determine its volume from its dimensions.

Volume of cuboid = length × width × height

The density is then calculated by dividing mass by volume.

4. Determining Density of an Irregular Solid

The volume of an irregular solid can be found using the displacement method. Water is placed in a measuring cylinder and the initial volume is recorded. The object is then completely immersed and the final volume is recorded.

Volume of object = Final volume − Initial volume

5. Relative Density

Relative density is the ratio of the density of a substance to the density of water at a specified reference condition. It has no unit because it is a ratio of two densities.

Relative Density = Density of substance ÷ Density of water

6. Density of Water

The commonly used approximate density of water is 1000 kg/m³ or 1 g/cm³.

7. Floating and Density

An object generally floats in a liquid when its average density is less than the density of the liquid. An object sinks when its average density is greater than the density of the liquid.

8. Applications of Density

Knowledge of density is useful in identifying materials, designing ships and aircraft, checking purity of substances, and selecting materials for different purposes.

💡 Worked Examples

Example 1: A metal block has a mass of 600 g and a volume of 200 cm³. Find its density.

Density = Mass ÷ Volume

= 600 ÷ 200

= 3 g/cm³

Example 2: An object has a mass of 2 kg and a volume of 0.001 m³. Find its density.

Density = m ÷ V

= 2 ÷ 0.001

= 2000 kg/m³

Example 3: Water in a measuring cylinder rises from 50 cm³ to 80 cm³ when an irregular stone is immersed. Find the volume of the stone.

Volume = Final volume − Initial volume

= 80 − 50

= 30 cm³

Example 4: A substance has a density of 800 kg/m³. Calculate its relative density.

Relative density = Density of substance ÷ Density of water

= 800 ÷ 1000

= 0.8

✅ Answers

1. Density is the mass contained in a unit volume of a substance.

2. Kilogram per cubic metre (kg/m³).

3. Density = Mass ÷ Volume

4. By using the displacement method with a measuring cylinder.

5. Relative density is the ratio of the density of a substance to the density of water.

6. Because it is a ratio of two quantities having the same unit, so the units cancel.

7. Density = 500 ÷ 250
= 2 g/cm³

8. Relative density = 1200 ÷ 1000
= 1.2

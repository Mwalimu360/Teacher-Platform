RATIOS AND PROPORTIONS

Form 1 Mathematics

📖 Notes

Meaning of Ratio

A ratio is a comparison of two or more quantities of the same kind. A ratio can be written using the symbol : or as a fraction.

Writing Ratios

If there are 6 boys and 3 girls, the ratio of boys to girls is 6:3. Dividing both terms by 3 gives 2:1.

Simplifying Ratios

To simplify a ratio, divide all its terms by their highest common factor.

Equivalent Ratios

Equivalent ratios have the same relationship between the quantities being compared.

Meaning of Proportion

A proportion is a statement that two ratios are equal.

Direct Proportion

Two quantities are directly proportional when an increase in one quantity causes a corresponding increase in the other quantity at a constant rate.

💡 Worked Examples

Example 1: Simplify the ratio 12:8.

Highest common factor of 12 and 8 is 4.
12 ÷ 4 : 8 ÷ 4
Answer = 3:2

Example 2: The ratio of boys to girls in a class is 3:2. If there are 15 boys, how many girls are there?

3 parts = 15 boys
1 part = 15 ÷ 3 = 5
Girls = 2 × 5
Answer = 10 girls

Example 3: If 2 books cost TSh 6,000, how much do 5 books cost at the same rate?

Cost of 1 book = 6,000 ÷ 2
= TSh 3,000
Cost of 5 books = 5 × 3,000
Answer = TSh 15,000

✅ Answers

2. 20:15 = 4:3

3. 2 parts = 10
1 part = 5
3 parts = 15 girls

4. 1 pen = 2,000 ÷ 4 = TSh 500
10 pens = 10 × 500
TSh 5,000
